Enemy Turn

👹
Total Enemy Attack ÷ Your Defense = Damage (rounded down) Example: 8 Attack ÷ 3 Defense = 2 damage

Enemies move and attack based on their stats using the same movement and range rules.

5

Line of Sight

👁️

You cannot attack or be attacked without a direct line of sight. Obstacles and enemies block vision.

6

Treasure Chests

Each level has a relic chest. Get in range and use Attack points to open it. Gain +1 to any stat for that level!
